About May

25 Years of Pattern Recognition

My career began in executive search, working across industries, leadership levels, and highly specialised roles, many of them newly emerging or difficult to define.

After thousands of interviews and leadership conversations, I began noticing recurring behavioural patterns: how people respond under pressure, how they influence others, where blind spots emerge, and the subtle signals that reveal how someone is likely to lead, decide, adapt, and grow in real environments.

Over time, that pattern recognition evolved into POV™ — an independent practice focused on behavioural assessment, reflective interviewing, and human centred leadership insight.

While much of my work has been with organisations and leaders, the same behavioural lens can also support emerging talent and graduates who are still discovering how they work, communicate, and grow.

  • 25 years in executive search and behavioural assessment
  • Experience with Predictive Index, Hogan, and Pearson assessment tools
  • BA in Modern Asian Studies, Griffith University, Australia
  • Works with organisations, leaders, founders, and emerging talent across different stages of growth
