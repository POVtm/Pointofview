A few practical questions

Before we begin.

Can we work with POV on just one service?

Yes. Each service can stand alone. We can also connect assessment with development, or design a Lab around an agreed team need, when that helps your goal.

Is this suitable for a small business?

Yes. The scope can focus on one critical hire, a first-time leader or a specific team challenge. We start with the decision or change you need and agree a proportionate approach.

Can our L&D team run the learning modules?

Yes. Design & Equip is built for internal delivery. We agree the facilitator materials, preparation, pilot support and intended use of the modules as part of the scope.

Will every engagement include a psychometric tool?

No. Tools are selected only where they add value to the brief. Relevant tool requirements and permissions are confirmed in advance, and results are interpreted alongside other evidence.

Who receives the findings?

We agree the audience and how information will be used before starting. Organisational assessment reports, team learning observations and private individual reflections have different purposes and sharing arrangements.
