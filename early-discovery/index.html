Find your stories

You don’t need twenty achievements.

A few real, specific stories can say far more. Part-time jobs, projects, internships, volunteering and everyday life all count.

Improved

What was happening before, what did you do, and what changed?

Figured out

What did you learn quickly, and how did you learn it?

Adapted to

What felt unfamiliar at first, and what helped you adjust?

Started

What did you notice or begin without being told?

Explained

Who did you help to understand, or bring along?

Don’t have a number? That’s fine. We never make things up. Describe what became easier, faster or clearer because of what you did.
